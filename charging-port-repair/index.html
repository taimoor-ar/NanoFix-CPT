Signs Your Charging Port Needs Fixing

Cable Wiggles to Charge

If you have to hold the cable at a certain angle to get a charge, the port pins are bent or worn.

Phone Won't Charge At All

Completely stopped charging? Could be a damaged port, debris blockage or loose connection inside.

Loose or Wobbly Cable

If the cable falls out easily, the port has physically worn out and needs replacing.

Charges Very Slowly

A damaged port can't transfer power efficiently, causing much slower charging than normal.

Charges On and Off

Intermittent charging — stops and starts — is a classic sign of a failing charging port.

PC Won't Detect Device

If your computer no longer recognises your phone when plugged in, the data pins in the port may be damaged.

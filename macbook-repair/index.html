Common MacBook Problems

Battery Drains Fast

macOS says "Service Recommended", the battery dies quickly or the MacBook shuts off while unplugged.

MacBook Won't Turn On

No lights, no chime, no sign of life. It could be the battery, charger, port or logic board. We diagnose before recommending anything.

Coffee or Water Spill

Switch it off straight away and don't charge it. Liquid damage gets worse with time, so act fast.

Cracked or Flickering Screen

Cracks, lines, dark patches or a display that flickers or goes black.

Won't Charge

Charger connected but the battery isn't charging. Often a worn port, cable or battery fault.

Runs Hot and Slow

Loud fans, high heat and sluggish performance. Sometimes dust or a failing battery is to blame.

Honest advice first. Some MacBook repairs, especially on newer Apple Silicon models, can cost a lot compared with the value of the laptop, and not every fault is repairable. We diagnose first, give you a quote and tell you plainly if a repair isn't worth it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does MacBook repair cost in Cape Town?

It depends on your MacBook model and the fault. Send us your model and the problem on WhatsApp and we'll give you a free quote before any work starts.

How do I know if my MacBook battery needs replacing?

Open System Settings, then Battery, and check battery health. If macOS says the battery needs service, it drains quickly or the MacBook shuts off while unplugged, it's time for a new battery.

My MacBook won't turn on. What should I try first?

Try a different charger and cable, leave it charging for 30 minutes, then hold the power button for 10 seconds. If there's still no sign of life, contact us for a diagnosis.

I spilled liquid on my MacBook. What should I do?

Switch it off immediately, unplug the charger and don't turn it on to test it. Message us as soon as you can. Liquid damage gets worse the longer it sits.

Will I lose my files during a repair?

Battery and screen repairs don't normally erase your files. We still recommend a backup if your MacBook starts. If the logic board has failed, we'll tell you honestly what's realistic.

Is there a warranty on MacBook repairs?

Yes. Repairs are covered by our 90-day warranty.

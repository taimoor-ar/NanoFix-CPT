Phone Got Wet? Do This Right Now

✅ Do

  • Take it out of the water immediately
  • Switch it off and leave it off
  • Remove the case, SIM tray and any cards
  • Wipe the outside with a dry, soft cloth
  • Contact a technician as soon as you can

❌ Don't

  • Don't plug it in to charge
  • Don't turn it on to "test" it
  • Don't use a hairdryer or put it near heat
  • Don't shake it or press buttons repeatedly
  • Don't rely on a bag of rice. It doesn't reliably fix water damage

Signs of Water Damage

Won't Turn On

Moisture can short the board or battery connection and stop the phone from starting.

Fog Behind the Lens or Screen

Moisture trapped behind the camera glass or display is a clear sign water got inside.

No Sound or Muffled Speaker

Water in the speaker or microphone causes crackling, low volume or no audio at all.

Not Charging

Moisture or corrosion in the charging port can stop charging or cause errors.

Screen Flickers or Glitches

Lines, flickering or ghost touches can appear hours or days after the water exposure.

Liquid Contact Indicator Red

Many phones have a small indicator inside the SIM slot that turns red or pink when wet.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if I drop my phone in water?

Take it out, switch it off and don't charge it. Remove the case and SIM tray, wipe the outside with a dry cloth and contact us straight away. Don't use a hairdryer or put it in rice.

Does putting a wet phone in rice work?

Not reliably. Rice doesn't pull moisture out of the inside of a phone and can leave dust in the ports. Switching it off and getting it checked quickly is the best thing you can do.

How much does water damage repair cost in Cape Town?

Water damage repair at NanoFix starts from R1,999. The price depends on your device and how much damage there is. We diagnose first and quote before any repair work.

Can a water damaged phone always be saved?

Not always. Saltwater, long exposure and charging a wet phone make recovery harder. We tell you honestly what's possible before you pay for a repair.

My phone seemed fine after it got wet. Do I still need it checked?

Yes. Corrosion can build up inside over days and cause problems later. A quick check can catch it early.

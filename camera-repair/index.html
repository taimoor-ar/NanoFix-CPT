Signs Your Phone Camera Needs Repair

Blurry or Out-of-Focus Photos

If photos stay blurry after cleaning the lens, the autofocus motor or camera module may be failing.

Black Screen in Camera App

The camera app opens but shows only black. This usually points to a damaged camera module or loose connection.

Cracked Camera Lens

A cracked lens glass causes streaks, spots and glare in every photo. It can also let dust and moisture in.

Spots or Dark Marks in Photos

Dust, moisture or damage inside the camera assembly shows up as permanent spots on your pictures.

Camera App Crashes or Freezes

Constant crashes or an error message when opening the camera can be a hardware fault in the camera module.

Camera Won't Switch or Focus

Can't switch between front and rear, or the camera hunts and never locks focus? We'll diagnose it on the spot.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does phone camera repair cost in Cape Town?

Camera repair at NanoFix starts from R899. The price depends on your device model and whether it's the front camera, rear camera or lens glass. WhatsApp us for a free quote.

Why are my phone photos blurry?

Often it's a dirty or cracked lens, a failing autofocus motor or a damaged camera module. Wipe the lens with a soft cloth first. If photos are still blurry, the camera needs repair.

Can you fix just the cracked lens glass?

Yes. If only the lens glass is cracked and the camera itself works fine, we can replace just the glass, which is usually cheaper than a full camera replacement.

Signs of a Motherboard Problem

Won't Turn On At All

No lights, no vibration, no response, even with a known-good charger and a fresh battery.

Won't Charge

If a new charging port and cable make no difference, the fault may be in the charging circuit on the board.

Stuck in a Boot Loop

The phone restarts over and over on the logo and never reaches the home screen.

No Display, But It's On

The phone vibrates or makes sounds but the screen stays black, even with a new screen fitted.

Died After Water Damage

Corrosion on the board is a common cause when a phone never recovers after getting wet.

Gets Very Hot or Shuts Off

Overheating, sudden shutdowns or a short circuit can point to a failing component on the board.

How do I know if it's the motherboard?

If the phone won't turn on or charge even with a new battery and charger, is stuck in a boot loop, or has no display after a new screen, the motherboard may be at fault. We confirm this with a proper diagnosis.

Is it worth repairing, or should I buy a new phone?

It depends on the age of the device, the repair cost and whether you need the data on it. We give you an honest recommendation after diagnosis, including when replacing makes more sense.

Can you recover my data?

Sometimes. If the board can be repaired, your data is usually still there. We can't promise it in every case, and we'll tell you what's realistic after diagnosis.

Signs Your Battery Needs Replacing

🔋 Dies before end of day

If your phone used to last all day but now needs charging by noon, the battery capacity has degraded.

⚡ Percentage jumps around

Battery showing 50% then suddenly dropping to 10%? The battery cells are failing and giving false readings.

📴 Shuts off unexpectedly

Phone switching off even with battery remaining is a classic sign of a worn-out battery.

🌡️ Gets very hot while charging

Excessive heat during charging can indicate a damaged battery that needs to be replaced urgently.

📱 Back cover bulging

A swollen battery is dangerous and needs immediate replacement. Don't ignore this one.

🐢 Phone running slow

iOS and Android throttle performance on low-health batteries. A new battery can make your phone feel brand new again.

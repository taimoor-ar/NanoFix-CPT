Common iPhone Problems We Fix

Cracked or Unresponsive Screen

Shattered glass, dead touch zones, lines or a black display after a drop.

Battery Draining Fast

Battery Health at around 80% or lower, sudden shutdowns or slow performance.

Won't Charge

Loose cable, charging on and off, or no charge at all. Often just a dirty or worn port.

Blurry or Black Camera

Cracked lens glass, failed autofocus or a black camera screen.

Water or Liquid Damage

Dropped in water, a pool or the sea? Switch it off and message us straight away.

Won't Turn On

Stuck on the Apple logo, boot loop or completely dead. We diagnose before recommending a repair.

Before We Arrive

Back Up If You Can

If your iPhone still works, back it up to iCloud or a computer. Repairs are usually safe, but a backup is always smart.

Know Your Passcode

We'll need your passcode to test the phone after the repair. You can change it afterwards.

Turn Off Find My iPhone

If you can, turn it off in Settings before the repair. It's easier to turn on again afterwards.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does iPhone repair cost in Cape Town?

Repairs start from R499 for a charging port, R649 for a battery and R949 for a screen. The exact price depends on your iPhone model. WhatsApp us your model for a free quote.

How do I know if my iPhone battery needs replacing?

Go to Settings, Battery, Battery Health. If maximum capacity is around 80% or lower, or the phone dies quickly or shuts off unexpectedly, a new battery will help.

Will my iPhone show a message about the replaced part?

Newer iPhones can show a notice in Settings when a part isn't from Apple. It doesn't stop the phone working. We'll explain what applies to your model before we start.

Will I lose my data during a repair?

Screen, battery and charging port repairs don't normally erase your data. We still recommend a backup if your phone works.

Is there a warranty on iPhone repairs?

Yes. Every repair comes with a 90-day warranty.
